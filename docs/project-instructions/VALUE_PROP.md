## Core Value Propositions (Universal)

### 1. Pre-Load Mental Stance
**What:** Identify challenges before they arrive and build responses proactively.

**Why It Matters:** Emotional hijacking happens when we're unprepared. Pre-loading reduces surprise's power over composure.

**How WellWell Delivers:**
- Morning Pulse ritual identifies day's hardest moment
- AI analyzes challenge and provides control map
- Personalized stance statement for the challenge
- Pre-emptive action to take before emotions cloud judgment

---

### 2. Real-Time Emotional Recalibration
**What:** When triggered, reframe immediately using Stoic principles.

**Why It Matters:** Suppressing emotions doesn't work. Reframing changes the judgment that causes suffering.

**How WellWell Delivers:**
- Intervene tool for real-time emotional support
- Intensity rating to acknowledge emotion
- AI-powered Stoic reframe based on virtue
- Immediate grounding action to take

---

### 3. Structured Reflection & Wisdom Extraction
**What:** Evening ritual to process the day and extract wisdom.

**Why It Matters:** Unprocessed experiences become rumination. Structured reflection turns experience into wisdom.

**How WellWell Delivers:**
- Evening Debrief with three structured questions
- AI synthesis of day's challenges and responses
- Virtue movement tracking (what improved, what didn't)
- Tomorrow's focus based on today's learnings

---

### 4. Measurable Virtue Growth
**What:** Track progress in four cardinal virtues over time.

**Why It Matters:** What gets measured gets improved. Quantitative tracking motivates continued practice.

**How WellWell Delivers:**
- Virtue scores (0-100) for Courage, Temperance, Justice, Wisdom
- Historical tracking with trends and deltas
- AI analysis maps challenges to relevant virtues
- Personalized recommendations to strengthen weakest virtues

## Unique Selling Propositions (USPs)

### 1. Pre-Load vs. React
Unlike meditation apps that help you calm down after stress, WellWell helps you prepare before stress arrives.

### 2. Human OS vs. Task OS
Unlike productivity tools that optimize your tasks, WellWell optimizes the human running the tasks.

### 3. Daily Practice vs. Weekly Sessions
Unlike therapy that happens weekly, WellWell provides daily support and practice.

### 4. Structured vs. Generic
Unlike self-help books with generic advice, WellWell provides personalized, AI-guided, structured practice.

### 5. Measurable vs. Subjective
Unlike other wellness tools, WellWell tracks quantitative virtue growth over time.
